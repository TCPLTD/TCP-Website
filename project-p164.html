First floor side extension, rear extensions and garage conversion

The project

Barnet Council approved a first floor side extension and part single, part two storey rear extensions replacing the old ones, with the garage converted into a habitable room, a new front bay window and canopy, and a raised patio.

An amendment then removed two gables from the front and added a lantern rooflight over the single storey rear extension.

Key features

  • First floor side extension
  • Part single, part two storey rear extension
  • Garage converted to a habitable room
  • Front bay window and canopy
  • Lantern rooflight
  • Raised patio
