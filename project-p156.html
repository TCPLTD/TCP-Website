New family house in Totteridge

Replacement two storey house with rooms in the roof and a basement, in a conservation area

Photos to come
Photo to come
Photo to come

The project

A replacement family house on a large plot in the Totteridge Conservation Area, an area of big detached houses set back behind mature trees. Earlier schemes for the site had been refused, so the design had to respect the character of the lane and the trees around it.

The approved scheme replaces the existing house with a two storey detached house with rooms in the roof space, a rear terrace and new hard and soft landscaping at the front. A second permission added a basement. The applications were supported by a heritage statement, ecological and bat surveys, an archaeological report and an arboricultural assessment.

After approval we worked through the final layouts with the client and coordinated the structural design, drainage and overheating assessment for the build.
