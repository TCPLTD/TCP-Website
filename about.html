About us

A small practice with a long record of approvals

Town & Country Planning Ltd was founded by Martin McGahon in 2005. From our base in Cockfosters we design and manage planning applications for homeowners, builders and developers across North London and Hertfordshire.

How we have grown

The practice has grown almost entirely through recommendation. Clients come back for their next project, and builders we work alongside introduce us to their customers.

We combine design with a detailed knowledge of local planning policy. That means proposals that suit the house and the street, and applications that are complete and clear when they reach the planning officer.

We stay involved after approval, preparing building control drawings and recommending builders and structural engineers we trust.
