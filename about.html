About us

Town & Country Planning Ltd is an independent architectural planning consultancy based in Cockfosters, Barnet, helping homeowners and developers across North London and Hertfordshire since 2009.

The practice was founded by Martin McGahon and has grown almost entirely through recommendation. We work mainly with homeowners who want to extend, convert or rebuild, and with developers and commercial clients on larger schemes. Whatever the size of the job, you get the same personal service.

Our strength is good design combined with a thorough knowledge of local planning policy and long working relationships with the councils we deal with. All drawings are produced in CAD, so builders, planners and building control officers can read them clearly.

We manage the whole process with the council and keep you informed at every stage. When it is time to build, we can recommend reliable builders and structural engineers we have worked with for years.
