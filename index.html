How we work

Five steps from idea to approval

How we work
  1. 1Consultation

    We meet you at the property, listen to what you want and tell you honestly what is likely to be possible, including anything you can build without planning permission.

  2. 2Measured survey

    We survey the house and plot in detail so every drawing starts from accurate measurements.

  3. 3Design

    We draw the existing and proposed plans in CAD and work through options with you until the design is right.

  4. 4Planning

    We prepare and submit the application, deal with the planning officer and keep you updated until a decision is issued.

  5. 5Building control

    Once approved, we produce the technical drawings and specification your builder and building control need.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

All questions
Do I need planning permission?

Not always. Many single storey rear extensions, loft conversions and garden rooms can be built without a full application, under what are called permitted development rights. It depends on your house, any earlier extensions and whether you are in a conservation area. We check this for you at the first visit.

How do we get started?

Call 07843 561129 or send us a message. We visit your home, listen to what you want and tell you honestly what is likely to be approved. Then we measure up, draw the plans and submit them.

How long does planning permission take?

Usually around eight weeks from when the council registers the application. We deal with the council throughout and keep you updated until the decision.

How much will it cost?

Every project is different, so we talk fees through with you in person and then give you a fixed quote. Call 07843 561129 for a chat.

Do you deal with the council for me?

Yes. We submit the application on your behalf and deal with the planning officer from start to finish, so you don't have to.

Do I need building regulations approval too?

Almost always, yes, even when you don't need planning permission. Once planning is approved, we prepare the building control drawings your builder needs.
