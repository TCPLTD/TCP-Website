About the practice

Good design, and the planning knowledge to get it approved.

You want more space, a better layout or a new home, and you want to know it will get through planning. We design something that works for you and suits your house, your street and the council's policies, then handle the application for you.

Most of our clients are homeowners planning their first extension or loft conversion, and we also work with builders, developers and commercial clients. We start by visiting the property, telling you honestly what is likely to be approved and what you may be able to build without planning permission at all.

From there the same small team measures, designs, submits and deals with the planning officer. You always know who you are talking to and where your application stands. Once you have approval, we prepare the building control drawings your builder needs.

How we work

Five steps from idea to approval

Householder applications are normally decided within eight weeks of the council validating them.

How we work
  1. 1Consultation

    We meet you at the property, listen to what you want and tell you honestly what is likely to be possible, including anything you can build without planning permission.

  2. 2Measured survey

    We survey the house and plot in detail so every drawing starts from accurate measurements.

  3. 3Design

    We draw the existing and proposed plans in CAD and work through options with you until the design is right.

  4. 4Planning

    We prepare and submit the application, deal with the planning officer and keep you updated until a decision is issued.

  5. 5Building control

    Once approved, we produce the technical drawings and specification your builder and building control need.

Frequently asked questions

All questions
Do I always need planning permission for an extension?

Not always. Many extensions can be built under permitted development rights. As a guide, a single storey rear extension can go out 3 metres on a terraced or semi-detached house and 4 metres on a detached house, or up to 6 and 8 metres through the council's prior approval process. These rights are restricted in conservation areas, on listed buildings and where earlier extensions have used them up. We check your position at the start.

How long does planning permission take?

Councils aim to decide householder applications within eight weeks of validating them, and major applications within 13 weeks. Some councils take longer in busy periods. We follow up with the planning officer for you and keep you updated.

Do you come to the property?

Yes. The first meeting is at your home, so we can see the house, the garden and the neighbours' properties, listen to what you want and tell you honestly what is likely to be possible.

How much will it cost?

Every project is different, so we talk fees through with you in person once we understand what you want to do, and then confirm a fixed quote. Council application fees are paid separately. Call 07843 561129 for a chat.

Can I see work you have done near me?

Yes. Our projects page shows hundreds of approved projects on a map and in a list, each linked to the council's record. Search by street, town or type of work.
