Extension and new roof in Radlett

Two storey side and rear extension with a new crown roof and loft rooms

Photos to come
Photo to come
Photo to come

The project

A large family extension in Radlett. We started with pre-application advice from Hertsmere, then applied for a part single, part two storey side and rear extension and a front porch.

The roof was rebuilt with a raised ridge and crown roof to create rooms in the loft, with two rear dormers with Juliet balconies and rooflights to the front, rear and sides. The scheme also includes a first floor rear balcony and new front landscaping. The application was supported by an arboricultural impact assessment, and Hertsmere approved it in November 2025.

Key features

  • Part single, part two storey side and rear extension
  • Front porch
  • Raised ridge with a crown roof
  • Two rear dormers with Juliet balconies
  • Rooflights
  • First floor rear balcony
