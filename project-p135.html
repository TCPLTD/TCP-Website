The project

The existing bungalow was demolished and replaced with a new bungalow with habitable rooms in the roof space, a detached garage, landscaping and bin and cycle stores. Harrow Council granted planning permission in 2024.

We then took the project through the planning conditions, including materials, site levels, drainage, landscaping and the fire safety statement, and a minor amendment to the shape and colour of the front windows.
