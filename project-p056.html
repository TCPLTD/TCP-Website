Family home extension in Radlett

Garage conversion, front and rear extensions, larger balcony and rear dormers

Photos to come

The project

Hertsmere granted permission to convert the garage to living space, add single storey front and rear extensions, enlarge the first floor balcony and add two rear dormers for more loft space.

As the project developed we gained further approvals for changes to the windows and materials, and for a larger balcony.

Key features

  • Garage converted to living space
  • Single storey front extension
  • Single storey rear extension
  • Enlarged first floor balcony
  • Two rear dormers
