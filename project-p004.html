Loft conversion and extension in Bushey

First floor front extension and raised ridge for a loft conversion

Photos to come
Photo to come
Photo to come

The project

Hertsmere granted permission for a partial garage conversion and a first floor front extension, with part of the ridge raised to make room for a loft conversion with two rear dormers and rooflights to the front and sides.

The scheme also includes new windows and new front landscaping and boundary treatment, with a second vehicle access.

Key features

  • Partial garage conversion
  • First floor front extension
  • Partly raised ridge for the loft
  • Two rear dormers
  • Rooflights
  • New driveway access

Planning decision

  • Approved 2026Partial conversion of garage to habitable space; First floor front extension including partial raised ridge height to facilitate loft conversion, with two rear dormers and insertion of one rooflight to front and each side elevation; Alterations to fenestration; Alterations to front hard and soft landscaping and boundary treatment, to facilitate additional vehicular access and crossover.Householder planning · Ref 26/0264/HSE View the council decision (opens in a new tab)
