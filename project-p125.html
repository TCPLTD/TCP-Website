Extensions and loft conversion in Mill Hill

Rear extension, new front porch and a loft conversion with side dormers

The project

Barnet Council approved a two storey front extension, a part single, part two storey rear extension and a roof extension with a dormer on each side, front and rear rooflights and a raised ridge.

The owners later decided to keep the original bay window, so we secured a revised permission in May 2023 (23/0993/HSE) that drops the front extension and adds a new front porch. The drawings show this final approved scheme.

Key features

  • Part single, part two storey rear extension
  • New front porch
  • Side dormers
  • Raised ridge height
  • Front and rear rooflights
