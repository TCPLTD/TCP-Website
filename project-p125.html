Extensions and loft conversion in Mill Hill

Two storey front and rear extensions and a loft conversion with side dormers

Photos to come

The project

Barnet Council approved a two storey front extension, a part single, part two storey rear extension and a roof extension with a dormer on each side, front and rear rooflights and a raised ridge.

The owners later decided to keep the original bay window, so we secured an amendment to the approved scheme with the internal changes that came with it.

Key features

  • Two storey front extension
  • Part single, part two storey rear extension
  • Side dormers
  • Raised ridge height
  • Front and rear rooflights
