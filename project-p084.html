Extension and loft conversion in Cockfosters

Rear extension, first floor side extension, garage conversion and hip to gable loft

Photos to come
Photo to come
Photo to come

The project

Enfield Council granted permission for a single storey rear extension and a first floor side extension over the existing single storey side extensions, with the garage converted into a habitable room.

The loft was extended to the side to form a gable, with a rear dormer, a Juliet balcony and front rooflights, confirmed as permitted development. A later amendment approved changes to the windows, skylights and sliding door.

Key features

  • Single storey rear extension
  • First floor side extension
  • Garage converted to a habitable room
  • Hip to gable loft with rear dormer
  • Juliet balcony
  • Front rooflights
