School alterations in Mill Hill

Internal alterations at a primary school, designed and taken through building control

The project

Internal alterations at a primary school in Mill Hill. We measured the building, drew the existing layout and developed several options with the school for the new layout, including the hall and kitchen.

The work involved forming a new door opening in a load-bearing wall with a steel beam, removing two walls and building a new one. We coordinated the structural design and fire plans and acted as principal designer for building control.

Key features

  • Primary school
  • New opening in a load-bearing wall
  • Steel beam
  • Walls removed and a new wall built
  • Building control approval
