Extensions, dormers and garden room in Hadley Wood

Side and rear extensions, front and rear dormers, a garden outhouse and a new front boundary

The project

A full remodel of a detached house in Hadley Wood. Enfield Council granted permission for a single storey side extension, a part single, part two storey rear extension, a front dormer and two rear dormers with rooflights. A garden outhouse followed under permitted development, confirmed with a lawful development certificate.

The new front wall, railings and gates took persistence. The first application was refused on height and the second because of a protected tree. We redesigned the scheme to stay clear of the tree's root protection area, and the third application was approved.

Key features

  • Part single, part two storey rear extension
