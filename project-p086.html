Side and rear extension and loft in Cockfosters

Single storey side and rear extension and a loft conversion

1 / 10

The project

Barnet Council granted permission for a single storey side and rear extension, replacing an older extension and infilling the patio, with a window replacing the garage door.

The loft was converted with a rear dormer, a Juliet balcony and four flat rooflights, confirmed as permitted development.

Key features

  • Single storey side and rear extension
  • Patio infilled
  • Garage door replaced with a window
  • Rear dormer with Juliet balcony
  • Four rooflights
