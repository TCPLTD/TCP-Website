A complete remodel in Hadley Wood

Front, side and rear extensions, garage conversion and a bigger roof, approved after a redesign

1 / 8

The project

A detached house in a quiet close in Hadley Wood, remodelled from front to back.

Our first application was refused by Enfield Council, which felt the extensions were too bulky for the close. We redesigned the scheme to keep the house in scale with its neighbours, and Enfield granted permission in April 2020 for a part single, part two storey front and rear extension with a new porch, a part first floor side extension, the garage extended and converted into a habitable room, and the roof extended to the side and rear with a raised ridge and rooflights.

After approval we prepared the construction drawings, worked with the structural engineer and produced the builder's set. The finished house has a long open plan kitchen and family room across the back opening onto a new terrace, a new hall and staircase, and balconies at the rear on the first floor.
