New homes on a former garage site in Southgate

Old council garages replaced with new homes, through the Mayor's Small Sites programme

Photos to come
Photo to come
Photo to come

The project

A row of disused council garages behind Ivy Road, put forward by the council through the Mayor of London's Small Sites programme to bring small plots forward for new homes.

We worked through pre-application advice with Enfield on several layouts before settling on three houses and a three storey block of flats, in a contemporary design with green sedum roofs, private amenity space, parking and cycle storage. Enfield's planning committee approved the scheme, followed by the detailed reserved matters.
