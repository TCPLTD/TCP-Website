Privacy

Privacy notice

How we use the information you give us, who we share it with and your rights.

Who we are

Town & Country Planning Ltd is a planning and design practice registered in England and Wales, company number 05448742. We are responsible for the personal information you give us. If you have any questions about it, call 07843 561129 or email martin@tcpltd.net.

What we collect

When you call, email or send an enquiry, we keep your name, your contact details, the address or area of the property and what you tell us about your project. If you become a client, we also hold the information we need to do the work, such as survey measurements, photographs and existing plans of the property.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, arrange a visit and give you a quote.
  • To design your project and prepare and manage planning and building control applications.
  • To keep the business and tax records the law requires.

We use your information because you have asked us to, to provide the service we have agreed with you, and to meet our legal obligations. We do not use it for marketing lists and we never sell it.

Who we share it with

Only those who need it for your project: the council's planning and building control teams, and, with your agreement, structural engineers or other consultants working on it. Councils publish planning applications on their websites, including the address and the drawings. That is part of the planning process and is controlled by the council.

Projects shown on this website

The projects on our map and project pages are approved planning applications, which are public records. We show the street and town only, never a house number or a client's name. Photographs are used with the owner's permission. If you would like a project removed, contact us and we will take it down.

This website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics. To show the site and the map, your browser loads fonts from Google Fonts and map images from OpenStreetMap, and a place search on the map is looked up with postcodes.io and OpenStreetMap. As with any website, these services see your device's internet address when they send the files.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries that do not go ahead for up to two years. We keep project records for as long as we may need them for legal and insurance reasons, usually up to 15 years after the work.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or delete it, or object to how we use it. Contact us using the details above. If you are not happy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.
