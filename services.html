Services

Everything from first sketch to building control

Whether it is a porch or a new house, we provide the design and planning work your project needs, and stay with it through building control.

01

Extensions

An extension is the most common way to gain space without moving. We design single and two storey rear extensions, side returns, wraparounds and over-garage extensions that suit the house and stand a strong chance of approval. Many single storey extensions can be built under permitted development; we check that first, because it can save you time and fees.

Includes

  • Single and two storey rear extensions
  • Side return and wraparound extensions
  • Over-garage extensions
  • Front porches
  • Kitchen and open-plan reconfiguration

02

Loft conversions

A loft conversion can add a bedroom and bathroom with little loss of garden. We design dormer, L-shaped dormer, hip to gable and mansard conversions, and advise where a loft can go ahead under permitted development and where an application is needed.

Includes

  • Rear dormers and L-shaped dormers
  • Hip to gable conversions
  • Mansard conversions
  • Rooflight conversions
  • Combined extension and loft schemes

See examples

Photo: loft conversions
03

New homes

We take replacement dwellings, new homes on residential plots and small developments from first sketches to planning consent, including the supporting statements councils expect, and appeals where a scheme has been refused.

Includes

  • Replacement dwellings
  • New homes on garden or infill plots
  • Small residential developments
  • Design and access statements
  • Planning appeals
  • Discharge of conditions

See examples

04

Conversions and change of use

Converting existing space is often the most cost-effective way to add a room. We prepare applications for garage and basement conversions, house to flat conversions and changes of use from commercial to residential.

Includes

  • Garage conversions
  • Basement conversions
  • House to flat conversions
  • Commercial to residential
  • Change of use applications

See examples

Photo: conversions and change of use
05

Garden rooms and outbuildings

Many garden offices and outbuildings do not need planning permission, depending on their size and position. We confirm what applies to your property and, where permission is needed, for example in a conservation area, we prepare and manage the application.

Includes

  • Garden offices and studios
  • Annexes
  • Pool houses
  • Stores and workshops

See examples

Photo: garden rooms and outbuildings
06

Planning advice and appeals

Sometimes the first step is advice rather than drawings. We assess the likely outcome of an application, suggest changes that would improve its chances, check permitted development rights and prepare planning statements. If a scheme has been refused, we advise on whether to revise it or appeal.

Includes

  • Pre-application advice
  • Permitted development checks
  • Planning and householder statements
  • Appeals to the Planning Inspectorate

See examples

Photo: planning advice and appeals
07

Building control drawings

After planning approval, the work needs building regulations approval. We prepare the detailed drawings and specification your builder needs to price and build correctly, coordinating structural design with engineers we work with regularly.

Includes

  • Building regulation drawings
  • Specification of materials
  • Structural coordination
  • Drainage layouts
  • Responses to building control queries
