Our services

From a single storey kitchen extension to a new home or a planning appeal, we provide the design and planning service your project needs.

Extensions and loft conversions

The most popular way to add space and value. Whether you want a larger kitchen, an extra bedroom or a home office, we design an extension that makes the best of your property and plot. Many extensions and lofts can be built under permitted development, so no planning application is needed. We check this at the start, which often saves time and cost. Where permission is needed, we manage the whole application.

See examples of our work

Includes

  • Single and double storey rear extensions
  • Side return and wraparound extensions
  • Over-garage extensions
  • Front porches
  • Dormer and L-shaped dormer lofts
  • Hip to gable and mansard lofts
  • Rooflight lofts

New build homes

Full design and planning for replacement dwellings and new homes, from first ideas to planning consent. We work on replacement houses on existing plots, new homes in large gardens and small developments, and prepare appeals to the Planning Inspectorate where an application has been refused.

See examples of our work

Includes

  • Replacement dwellings
  • New homes on residential plots
  • Pre-application advice
  • Design and access statements
  • Planning appeals
  • Discharge of conditions

Garage and flat conversions

Converting an existing space is often the most cost-effective way to add a room. Many garage conversions fall under permitted development. We also prepare applications for house to flat conversions and changes of use across Barnet, Enfield and neighbouring boroughs.

See examples of our work

Includes

  • Integral and detached garage conversions
  • Basement conversions
  • House to flat conversions
  • Commercial to residential
  • Change of use applications

Garden offices and outbuildings

Many garden offices and outbuildings can be built without planning permission, depending on size and position. We check your property and advise. In conservation areas or on listed buildings, we prepare and manage the full application.

See examples of our work

Includes

  • Garden offices and studios
  • Annexes
  • Pool buildings
  • Stores and workshops

Planning consultancy

Sometimes you need advice before you commit to drawings. We advise on the likely outcome of an application, on design changes that would improve its chances, and on your permitted development rights, and we prepare the supporting documents.

See examples of our work

Includes

  • Pre-application advice
  • Permitted development checks
  • Planning and householder statements
  • Appeal support
  • Commercial development advice

Building control drawings

After planning approval, building regulations approval is the next step. We prepare detailed drawings and specifications for building control so your builder can price and build the work correctly, coordinating with structural engineers where needed.

See examples of our work

Includes

  • Building regulation drawings
  • Specification of materials
  • Structural coordination
  • Drainage design
  • Submission and responses to building control
